OPTIONS

Approx. distance: --
Caps how many monsters can be alive at once from a single wave. Normal monsters spawn individually first, then bosses, then superbosses. Whatever doesn't fit gets merged into fewer, tougher monsters instead (shown with an x2/x3/etc. multiplier). Ultrabosses always spawn individually. Lower this on slower devices to reduce lag.
Changing the seed reloads the page into a fresh world.
Click the screen to look around - WASD to walk - Space to jump - Shift to sprint - hold Left Click to mine - hold Right Click (or Tab) to place - right-click the crafter to open it - 1-6 to select hotbar slot - G to open inventory (drag items between slots, drag onto an equipment slot to equip) - look at a chest and press G to open it - N to send the next wave - E to close options / free-lock the cursor

WIDE FOV WARNING

Pushing the field of view past 160° switches to a strong Panini projection. It bends straight lines near the edges of the screen and can cause eye strain or motion sickness for some players.

The upside: the extra wraparound view can let you spot monsters approaching from behind you.
