## Core Gameplay Loop

- The game is first person. You explore the triangular world and see all the biomes, swim in the water, then come back to your base for the main part of the game below.
- Endless monster survival. Think of it like Zombs.io - you upgrade, you fight, you skip, build out strategies that work to fight the monsters, and make it to the next tiers. Main game progression actually stops at 30,000 waves, but NG+ has a special item relevant for 340,000.
- When you explore, you can mine all the terrain and stuff and use those items to come back and make new gear to fight off the bad guys. They're all cubes, by the way.
- The entire game has hundreds of items, with multiple builds per main tier of progression, strategy, inventory management, all of it.

## World & Biomes

- Each biome has a distinct color and hardness, and terrain shape, along with it's own special song.
- Monsters can eat through the world, so you'll have to rebuild your central mountain every once in a while. You can mine faster by wielding stronger triangles (found deeper underground).
- You can place chests, with only 6 slots, for an expensive price. And monsters can eat through them and delete your items. It's much safer to store things in your inventory.

## Monsters & Bosses

- Every 10, 100, 1000 waves you'll see a different type of boss. The 1000 wave guy doesn't mess around. Good luck xD
- Every monster has their own type, so look out for different ones (If you see an Elite Ultraboss im so sorry and hope to god its not on wave 1000 LOL)
- Many items have special mechanics, and if I can give you any sort of hint, look for items that change their name from the previous versions of themselves. That usually signifies a major power boost or ability, which will help you clear these monsters.

## Items, Weapons & Armor

- Theres more than 100 different items. This is armor sets, multiple weapon lines, all with special abilties. You got to find what works as the set for you for your wave.
- Each tier can have special bonuses that do good things for you so look out for the good stuff. Could be OP, you never know. Craft everything.
- Every item has a small bit of the game's lore. Put them together, and you'll get the story. The entire thing is revealed when you combine all the top tier items into the one singularity item, which is appropriately named for being, well, a singularity item.
- Special abilities are tied to your gear. So choose wisely.

## Systems & Polish

- The game should autosave and autoload for you but you can export your save. Dont you dare edit the numbers in your save!! Grrr..
- If you press backslash, you'll get the developer console. In here is.. some cheaty stuff, if you can find the right command (Remember The Stanley Parable?)
- Don't worry about exploding your laptop. The game's only like 300 kilobytes in it's compressed form and the math is not that heavy. If your pc REALLY has a problem, turn on Monster Consolidation.
- I used Bytebeat music here in the game to embed music without adding megabytes of data to the file. So shout out to the people who make Bytebeat. They are smarter than I am.
- Options menu has a lot of sliders and toggles so you can actually customize the game how you want (or just turn them all down for a potato)
- The end has a multi minute cutscene. Yes. Go find it. So fun!
